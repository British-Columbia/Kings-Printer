<h1>LEGAL PROFESSION (AMENDMENT) ACT</h1>

<h2><small>[SBC 2025] CHAPTER 4</small></h2>

**Effective June 5, 2025**

<hr/>

</div>

HIS MAJESTY, by and with the advice and consent of the Legislative Assembly of the Province of British Columbia, enacts as follows:

<div align="center">
<i>Contracts Act</i>
</div>

#### Amendments related to the prevention of litigation

1. Section 2 of the [*Contracts Act*](../../Laws/Contracts%20Act/Consolidated.md), [SBC 2024, c. 22](../2024/22.md), is amended

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) by striking out “required of them.” and substituting “required of them;”, and

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) by adding the following definition:

    > “settlement agreement” means a contract made for the sole purpose of a plaintiff voluntarily dismissing litigation or prosecution in return for one or more concessions on the part of the defendant(s). .

2. Section 8 is amended by striking out “by said contract.” and substituting “by said contract, or if the contract is a settlement agreement.”.



<div align="center">
<i>Law Society Act</i>
</div>

#### Amendments related to the Act as a whole



3. The [Law Society Act](../../Laws/Law%20Society%20Act/Consolidated.md), [SBC 2024, c. 14](../2024/14.md), is amended

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) by striking out “President” wherever it appears and substituting “Executive Director”, and

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) by striking out “Law Society of British Columbia” wherever it appears and substituting “Society”.

4. Section 2 is amended

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) by striking out “law.” and substituting “law;”, and

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) by adding the following definition:

    > “Society” means the Law Society of British Columbia. .



#### Amendments related to the Board of Benchers

5. Section 7 is repealed.

6. Section 8 is amended

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) by repealing paragraphs (a) and (b),

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) in paragraph (d) by striking out “two other Benchers” and substituting “four other Benchers”,

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(c) in paragraph (d) by striking out “Part 4 of this Act.” and substituting “Part 4 of this Act;”, and

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(d) by adding the following paragraph:

    > (e) two other Benchers nominated by the Premier.



#### Amendments related to law licenses



7. Section 4 is amended by striking out “the issuance and management of law licenses and law firm licenses” and substituting “admitting individuals to the bar to become members of the Law Society, registering law firms”.

8. Section 11 is amended by striking out “certified” and substituting “admitted to the bar”.

9. Section 12 is amended

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) by striking out “possess a law license issued by the Law Society of British Columbia” and substituting “be a member of the Society”, and

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) by amending subsection (a) by striking out “issue a law license” and substituting “admit an individual into the Society as a member”.

10. Section 13 is amended by striking out “possessing a law license” and substituting “who are members of the Society”.

11. Section 14 is amended by striking out “a law license” and substituting “membership within the Society”.

12. The title of Part 5 is amended by striking out “Licenses” and substituting “Registration”.

13. Section 27 is amended by striking out “possess a law firm license issued by the Law Society of British Columbia” and substituting “be registered with the Society”.

14. Section 28 is amended by striking out “a law firm license” and substituting “the registration of a law firm”.

15. Section 29 is amended by striking out “a law firm license” and substituting “a law firm from practicing”.

16. Sections 30 and 31 are amended by striking out “the continuation or issuance of a law firm license” and substituting “accepting a law firm's registration application, or continuing the registration of the said law firm”.

17. Sections 32 and 34 are amended

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) by striking out “a law license” and substituting “a Society member's membership”, and

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) by striking out “said law license” and substituting “said Society membership”.

18. Section 33 is amended

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) by striking out “a law license” and substituting “a Society member's membership”, and

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) by striking out “the law license” and substituting “the said Society membership”.

19. Section 35 is amended

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) by striking out “their law license” wherever it appears and substituting “their Society membership”, and

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) by striking out “holding a law license” and substituting “being a member of the Law Society of British Columbia”.



#### Amendments related to elections



20. Section 15 is amended

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) by adding “that does not have suspended Society membership” after “Every member of the Society”, and

    &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) by striking out “shall be entitled to vote once in any election” and substituting “shall be entitled to vote in any election,”.

21. Section 16 is amended by adding “that does not have suspended Society membership” after “Every member of the Society”.

22. Section 17 is repealed.

23. Section 18 is amended by striking out “1st of January, the 1st of April, the 1st of July, and the 1st of October” and substituting “1st of July, 1st of November, and the 1st of March”.

24. Section 21 is amended by striking out “through a voting process determined by the President” and substituting “as determined by the Board of Benchers, in line with this Act”.



#### Amendments related to discipline



25. Section 34 (a) is amended by striking out “the Board of Benchers” and substituting “at least three members of the Society chosen by the Executive Director”.

26. Section 38 is amended by striking out “court of law” and substituting “the Provincial Court”.



<div align="center">
<i>Offences By-law</i>
</div>

#### Amendments related to the crimes in the legal profession

27. Section 127 (b) of the [Offences By-law](../../Laws/Offences%20By-law/Consolidated.md), [C.B. 116](../../CB/116.pdf), is repealed and the following substituted:
> (b) practices law without the permission of the Law Society of British Columbia or a judge.

28. Section 128 is amended by striking out “under the authority of the City of Vancouver.” and substituting “under the authority of the province of British Columbia, or, in the case of a business which practices law, fails to be registered with the Law Society of British Columbia.”.



#### Commencement

29. This Act comes into force on the date of Royal Assent.
